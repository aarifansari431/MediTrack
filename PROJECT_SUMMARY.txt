================================================================================
VALIDATION RULES
================================================================================

Doctor Registration:
  - Name: 2-100 characters
  - Email: Valid email format
  - Phone: 10-15 digits
  - Consultation Fee: Positive amount
  - Experience: Non-negative integer
  - Max Patients: Positive integer

Patient Registration:
  - Name: 2-100 characters
  - Age: 0-150 years
  - Gender: MALE/FEMALE/OTHER
  - Blood Group: A+/A-/B+/B-/AB+/AB-/O+/O-
  - Email: Valid email format
  - Phone: 10-15 digits

Appointment Booking:
  - Cannot be in the past
  - Doctor must be active
  - Patient must be active
  - Doctor must be available on the day
  - Doctor slots must be available
  - Prevent duplicate bookings
